# Sandbox.GamepadCode

Game controller codes, driven from SDL.

- **Kind:** enum
- **Namespace:** `Sandbox`
- **Assembly:** `Sandbox.Engine`
- **Base:** `System.Enum`

## Values

- `None`
- `A`
- `B`
- `X`
- `Y`
- `SwitchLeftMenu` - Normally the small button on the left side of a gamepad
- `Guide` - The big button in the middle of a gamepad, usually with the logo on it
- `SwitchRightMenu` - This is automatically used as the escape key in all games
- `LeftJoystickButton` - The button when you press down on the stick
- `RightJoystickButton` - The button when you press down on the stick
- `SwitchLeftBumper` - Also known as the left bumper, or LB, or L1
- `SwitchRightBumper` - Also known as the right bumper, or RB, or R1
- `DpadNorth`
- `DpadSouth`
- `DpadWest`
- `DpadEast`
- `Misc1` - This is a button that doesn't have a specific name, like the share button on some controllers
- `Paddle1` - Extra button on the back of some gamepads, like the Xbox Elite
- `Paddle2` - Extra button on the back of some gamepads, like the Xbox Elite
- `Paddle3` - Extra button on the back of some gamepads, like the Xbox Elite
- `Paddle4` - Extra button on the back of some gamepads, like the Xbox Elite
- `Touchpad`
- `BUTTONS_MAX`
- `LeftTrigger` - Also known as LT, or L2
- `RightTrigger` - Also known as RT, or R2
